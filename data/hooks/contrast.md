# Contrast, myths and opinions

Hooks that put two things side by side, or push against what the viewer already believes.
Contrast is easy to see in two seconds; the risk is sounding like clickbait, so the video must
back the claim with something on screen.

## Myth vs fact

**Template:** "Myth: [common belief]. Fact: [what is true]."

**Examples:**
- en: "Myth: Samarkand is only for history fans."
- uz: "Afsona: Samarqand faqat tarix ixlosmandlari uchun."
- ru: "Миф: Самарканд только для любителей истории."

**When to use:** topics where many people repeat a belief you can correct with evidence.

**Why it works:** correcting a belief the viewer may hold feels personal and useful at once.

## Expectation vs reality

**Template:** "What I expected [place / thing] to be vs what it really is."

**Examples:**
- en: "What I expected from Chorsu bazaar vs reality."
- uz: "Chorsu bozoridan nima kutgandim va nima ko'rdim."
- ru: "Чего я ждал от Чорсу и что увидел на самом деле."

**When to use:** travel, products and experiences with a clear gap between image and truth (either direction).

**Why it works:** two contrasting shots are readable in a second, and the honesty of "reality" earns trust.

## End first

**Template:** "This was [very different state] [short time] ago."

**Examples:**
- en: "Two hours ago this was just flour and water."
- uz: "Ikki soat oldin bu shunchaki un va suv edi."
- ru: "Два часа назад это была просто мука и вода."

**When to use:** cooking, crafts, renovations: any process with a satisfying result.

**Why it works:** showing the finished result first creates a "how?" question that the process then answers.

## You've been doing it wrong

**Template:** "You've been [doing everyday thing] wrong."

**Examples:**
- en: "You've been pouring your tea wrong."
- uz: "Choyni shu paytgacha noto'g'ri quyib kelgansiz."
- ru: "Вы всю жизнь неправильно наливаете чай."

**When to use:** everyday actions with a better method you can show.

**Why it works:** a mild challenge to competence is hard to ignore; viewers stay to check whether it applies to them.

**Watch out:** the "right way" must be real and visible, or the comments will turn on you.

## Unpopular opinion

**Template:** "Unpopular opinion: [mild, defensible claim]."

**Examples:**
- en: "Unpopular opinion: autumn is Tashkent's best season."
- uz: "Ko'pchilik rozi bo'lmaydi: Toshkentning eng zo'r fasli kuz."
- ru: "Непопулярное мнение: осень лучшее время в Ташкенте."

**When to use:** a creator or brand with a voice; topics where people already have preferences.

**Why it works:** disagreement drives comments, and comments push the video to more people.

**Watch out:** keep it mild and about taste, never about people or groups.

## Tourist vs local

**Template:** "Tourists [do X]. Locals [do Y]."

**Examples:**
- en: "Tourists eat here. Locals eat two streets away."
- uz: "Turistlar shu yerda ovqatlanadi. Mahalliylar esa narida."
- ru: "Туристы едят здесь. Местные за углом."

**When to use:** travel and city content, especially food and prices.

**Why it works:** it promises the authentic version and lets both groups feel addressed: visitors want it, locals want to check it.

## Then vs now

**Template:** "[Place] [years] ago vs today."

**Examples:**
- en: "Tashkent's Amir Temur square, then and now."
- uz: "Amir Temur xiyoboni: avval va hozir."
- ru: "Сквер Амира Темура: тогда и сейчас."

**When to use:** places, prices, technology or habits that changed visibly; needs an old photo or clip.

**Why it works:** change over time is a story in two frames, and it pulls in nostalgia from older viewers.

## Stop doing this

**Template:** "Stop [common habit]. Do [better habit] instead."

**Examples:**
- en: "Stop washing rice once. Do this instead."
- uz: "Guruchni bir marta yuvishni bas qiling."
- ru: "Хватит промывать рис один раз."

**When to use:** practical tips where a common habit has a better alternative.

**Why it works:** a direct command is a pattern interrupt, and the "instead" promises a quick fix.

## Pick a side

**Template:** "[Option A] or [option B]? There's only one right answer."

**Examples:**
- en: "Samsa from the tandoor or the oven? Choose."
- uz: "Tandir somsa yoki duxovka somsa? Bittasini tanlang."
- ru: "Самса из тандыра или из духовки? Выбирайте."

**When to use:** friendly rivalries (food, cities, seasons) where people enjoy arguing.

**Why it works:** asking viewers to choose turns watching into taking part, and the comments become the second half of the video.
