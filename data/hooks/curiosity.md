# Curiosity hooks

Hooks that open a question the viewer wants closed. The video must actually answer it, or the
viewer feels tricked and the comments say so.

## Open loop

**Template:** "[Surprising fact]. Why? I'll show you at the end."

**Examples:**
- en: "This café has no menu. The reason is genius."
- uz: "Bu kafeda menyu yo'q. Sababini oxirida aytaman."
- ru: "В этом кафе нет меню. Причина вас удивит."

**When to use:** a topic with one strange, concrete fact you can explain in the last scenes.

**Why it works:** an unanswered question is uncomfortable; people stay to close it. Delaying the answer turns 3 seconds of attention into 30.

## The hidden detail

**Template:** "Everyone walks past [place or thing]. Almost nobody notices [detail]."

**Examples:**
- en: "Millions walk past this gate. Few look up."
- uz: "Hamma bu darvozadan o'tadi. Tepaga kam odam qaraydi."
- ru: "Все проходят мимо этих ворот. Наверх смотрят единицы."

**When to use:** landmarks, familiar streets, everyday objects with a story in a small detail.

**Why it works:** it promises the viewer an advantage over everyone else: they will see what others miss.

## Nobody talks about this

**Template:** "Nobody tells you this about [topic]."

**Examples:**
- en: "Nobody tells you this about moving to Tashkent."
- uz: "Toshkentga ko'chishdan oldin buni hech kim aytmaydi."
- ru: "Об этом в Ташкенте никто не рассказывает."

**When to use:** practical topics where the usual advice leaves out something real (costs, timing, local rules).

**Why it works:** it signals insider knowledge and frames the video as a correction to everything else the viewer has seen.

## Guess first

**Template:** "Can you guess [what / how much / where] this is?"

**Examples:**
- en: "Guess what this machine makes. Wrong, probably."
- uz: "Bu mashina nima qiladi? Topa olasizmi?"
- ru: "Угадайте, что делает эта машина."

**When to use:** an object, dish or place that looks puzzling in the first frame.

**Why it works:** a guess is a small commitment; once viewers have guessed, they stay to check if they were right, and many comment their guess.

## What happens if

**Template:** "What happens if you [do X] for [time]?"

**Examples:**
- en: "What happens if you drink green tea every morning?"
- uz: "Har kuni ertalab ko'k choy ichsangiz nima bo'ladi?"
- ru: "Что будет, если месяц пить зелёный чай?"

**When to use:** habits, experiments, small challenges with a visible result.

**Why it works:** it sets up a cause and an outcome; the brain wants the outcome before it moves on.

## The odd object

**Template:** "Why does [place] have [unusual number] of [object]?"

**Examples:**
- en: "Why does this bazaar sell 40 kinds of raisins?"
- uz: "Nega bu bozorda 40 xil mayiz sotiladi?"
- ru: "Почему на этом базаре 40 видов изюма?"

**When to use:** markets, workshops, collections, anything with a striking quantity on camera.

**Why it works:** the number makes the claim concrete and checkable, and the "why" opens a loop.

## Your first guess is wrong

**Template:** "You think it's [obvious answer]. It isn't."

**Examples:**
- en: "You think plov's secret is the meat. It isn't."
- uz: "Oshning siri go'shtda deb o'ylaysizmi? Yo'q."
- ru: "Думаете, секрет плова в мясе? Нет."

**When to use:** food, craft and how-to topics where the real key is a less obvious step.

**Why it works:** it contradicts the viewer's own prediction, which is more personal than a general surprise.

## The insider secret

**Template:** "[Profession] rarely say this, but [secret]."

**Examples:**
- en: "Baristas rarely admit this about your latte."
- uz: "Baristalar buni kamdan-kam aytishadi."
- ru: "Бариста редко говорят об этом вслух."

**When to use:** when the video shows a real professional or a behind-the-counter view.

**Why it works:** it offers access to a closed group's knowledge; the person on camera lends credibility.

## Save the best for last

**Template:** "[Number] [things] in [place], and the last one is the best."

**Examples:**
- en: "Four breakfasts in Tashkent. The last one wins."
- uz: "Toshkentdagi to'rtta nonushta. Eng zo'ri oxirida."
- ru: "Четыре завтрака в Ташкенте. Лучший в конце."

**When to use:** list videos with one clear favourite.

**Why it works:** it tells viewers there is a reward at the end, so every earlier item is a step toward it, not a reason to swipe.
