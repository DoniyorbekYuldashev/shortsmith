# Story, POV and people

Hooks that start a story or put the viewer inside one. A face, a person or "I" in the first
second makes a video feel like it was made by someone, not for an algorithm.

## POV

**Template:** "POV: you [specific situation]."

**Examples:**
- en: "POV: you found Tashkent's quietest café on a rainy day."
- uz: "POV: yomg'irli kunda Toshkentning eng sokin kafesini topdingiz."
- ru: "POV: вы нашли самое тихое кафе Ташкента в дождь."

**When to use:** atmosphere and experience videos; first-person camera footage.

**Why it works:** "you" puts the viewer in the scene, so the video becomes their moment instead of someone else's.

## I tried it

**Template:** "I tried [X] for [time]. Here's what happened."

**Examples:**
- en: "I ate only street food in Tashkent for 3 days."
- uz: "3 kun faqat ko'cha ovqati yedim. Natija?"
- ru: "Три дня ел только уличную еду в Ташкенте."

**When to use:** experiments, challenges, product tests with a clear result.

**Why it works:** a personal experiment has stakes and an ending, and the viewer gets the result without the effort.

## A day in the life

**Template:** "A day in the life of a [job] in [place]."

**Examples:**
- en: "A day in the life of a Samarkand bread baker."
- uz: "Samarqandlik novvoyning bir kuni."
- ru: "Один день из жизни самаркандского пекаря."

**When to use:** jobs, crafts and routines that viewers rarely see from the inside.

**Why it works:** curiosity about other people's lives is endless, and real routines feel honest.

## Start in the middle

**Template:** "...and that's when [unexpected event] happened."

**Examples:**
- en: "And that's when the whole bazaar went silent."
- uz: "Shunda butun bozor jim bo'lib qoldi."
- ru: "И тут весь базар замолчал."

**When to use:** a story with one dramatic moment; jump to it, then rewind.

**Why it works:** opening at the peak skips the slow setup, and the viewer stays to learn how it got there.

## Small beginnings

**Template:** "This [business / place] started with [tiny thing]."

**Examples:**
- en: "This bakery started with one tandoor and a loan."
- uz: "Bu novvoyxona bitta tandir va qarzdan boshlangan."
- ru: "Эта пекарня началась с одного тандыра."

**When to use:** brand stories, local businesses, founders.

**Why it works:** a small start makes success relatable, and people like to support those who began like them.

## The mistake so you don't

**Template:** "I [lost money / time] on [X], so you don't have to."

**Examples:**
- en: "I overpaid for a taxi in Tashkent. Here's how not to."
- uz: "Taksiga ortiqcha to'ladim. Siz to'lamang."
- ru: "Я переплатил за такси. Вот как не повторить."

**When to use:** travel tips, purchases, beginner advice.

**Why it works:** admitting a mistake builds trust, and avoiding a loss motivates more than gaining something.

## What a stranger told me

**Template:** "A [stranger] at [place] told me [surprising advice]."

**Examples:**
- en: "An old man at the bazaar told me how to pick melons."
- uz: "Bozordagi bir otaxon qovun tanlashni o'rgatdi."
- ru: "Старик на базаре научил меня выбирать дыню."

**When to use:** travel and culture; advice that comes from a real encounter.

**Why it works:** wisdom from a stranger feels authentic, local and slightly mysterious.

## They said it wouldn't work

**Template:** "Everyone said [X] would fail. Look at it now."

**Examples:**
- en: "Everyone said a café on this street would close."
- uz: "Hamma bu ko'chadagi kafe yopiladi degandi."
- ru: "Все говорили, что кафе здесь закроется."

**When to use:** founder stories, comebacks, unusual choices that paid off.

**Why it works:** the underdog arc is one of the oldest story shapes; viewers want to see the proof.

## First time trying

**Template:** "First time trying [local thing] as a [person]."

**Examples:**
- en: "First time trying kurt. My face says everything."
- uz: "Chet ellik do'stim birinchi marta qurt tatib ko'rdi."
- ru: "Мой друг из Европы впервые пробует курт."

**When to use:** food and culture content with a visible reaction.

**Why it works:** genuine reactions are contagious, and locals enjoy seeing their culture through new eyes.
